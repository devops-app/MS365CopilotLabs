## Introduction

---

## Prompting Best Practices

- Be specific about the role Copilot should play, such as senior business analyst, project manager, HSE advisor, or finance analyst.
- Provide clear source context, such as this document, this email thread, this workbook, or this meeting transcript.
- State the required format, such as table, executive summary, action tracker, slide outline, risk register, or email draft.
- Define the audience and tone, such as senior leadership, project team, supplier, or cross-functional stakeholders.
- Ask Copilot to include risks, actions, owners, due dates, assumptions, and recommendations where appropriate.
- Always review Copilot output for accuracy, confidentiality, compliance, and business context before sending or publishing.

## Microsoft Word Prompts

Use these prompts in Word to create structured reports, summarize documents, draft formal notes, and convert working material into professional business documents.

| Use Case | Copy-ready Copilot Prompt | Recommended Output |
|---|---|---|
| Project Status Report | Create a professional weekly project status report based on the information in this document. Use concise business language suitable for senior management. | Structured status report |
| Technical Document Summary | Summarize this document into 1 page for a business audience. Include: Purpose, Key Findings, Risks, Recommendations, and Actions Required. Avoid technical jargon where possible. | One-page summary |
| Meeting Minutes | Convert these notes into formal meeting minutes. Include: Meeting Objective, Attendees, Key Discussion Points, Decisions Made, Action Items, Owners, and Due Dates. Format professionally. | Formal minutes |
| Risk Assessment | Review this document and identify operational, safety, commercial, and schedule risks. Create a risk register with Risk Description, Impact, Likelihood, Mitigation Actions, and Risk Owner. | Risk register |
| HSE Incident Summary | Create an executive-level HSE incident summary from this document. Include: Incident Overview, Root Cause, Immediate Actions Taken, Corrective Actions, Preventive Measures, and Lessons Learned. | Executive HSE summary |

## Microsoft Outlook Prompts

|---|---|---|
| Executive Email Summary | Summarize all emails received in the last 7 days related to this project. Group into Decisions, Risks, Actions Required, and Stakeholder Requests. Highlight urgent items. | Email digest |

Prioritize them as High, Medium, or Low based on deadline, sender importance, business impact, risk, and required action. Present the result in a table with Sender, Subject, Reason for Priority, Required Action, Due Date, and Suggested Response. | Priority inbox table |
