# Lab-to-Sample Data Alignment Guide

[← Back to Home](../index.md)

This guide helps facilitators confirm that each lab has the correct supporting sample data or training asset before delivery. The Excel workbook directly supports the Excel and Analyst activities, while some communication and agent labs require additional Word, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook, or SharePoint sample materials.

| Lab | Excel sample use | Required sheet or asset | Alignment notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab 1 - Copilot Chat | Optional | Scenario_Guide | Can use the workbook scenario as context, but the lab mainly focuses on brainstorming, briefing structure, and validation against approved sources. |
| Lab 2 - Word | Indirect | BP Refinery Turnaround Readiness Briefing.docx; Scenario_Guide | The Excel workbook can provide operational context, but the Word lab still needs a separate briefing document or prompt-based scenario for turnaround readiness. |
| Lab 3 - Excel | Direct | Operational_Data; Data_Dictionary; Prompt_Practice | Primary lab for the workbook. Fields should support trend summaries, regional comparison, line charts, outlier review, and management-ready insights. |
| Lab 4 - PowerPoint | Partial | Expected_Insights; BP Regional Operations Review Template.pptx | Use Excel findings and expected insights to shape slides on operational performance, safety focus areas, emissions trends, and recommended actions. |
| Lab 5 - Teams | Indirect | Sample meeting agenda, meeting chat, or transcript | The workbook can provide background metrics, but Teams preparation and recap require a sample meeting record with decisions, risks, owners, and due dates. |
| Lab 6 - Outlook | Indirect | Sample terminal maintenance email thread | The workbook may support the business context, but Outlook tasks need sample emails covering the issue, missing information, stakeholder response, and follow-up meeting request. |
| Lab 7 - Researcher and Analyst agents | Direct for Analyst; indirect for Researcher | Operational_Data; Expected_Insights; approved documents and communications | Analyst agent uses the structured dataset directly. Researcher agent needs additional approved documents, emails, or meeting content to summarize decisions, risks, and next steps. |
| Lab 8 - Agent Builder | Reference only | Approved maintenance checklist, safety permit checklist, stakeholder contact list, escalation matrix | The workbook is not sufficient as an agent knowledge source. The agent requires approved procedural and governance content. |
| Lab 9 - Copilot Studio | Reference only | Approved operational guidance, HSE reminders, training resources, escalation contacts | Use the Excel scenario for context only. The Copilot Studio agent should be grounded in approved knowledge sources and clear guardrails. |
| Capstone Activity | Direct and supporting | Operational_Data; Expected_Insights; Word briefing; PowerPoint template; meeting and email samples | Requires the full sample asset set so learners can create the operational readiness package across Chat, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook, and agent tools. |
