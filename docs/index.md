# Microsoft 365 Copilot and Agent Exercises

These hands-on exercises adapt the Microsoft 365 Copilot and agent lab-guide style for a company business context. The scenarios focus on common energy-industry workflows such as operational performance, safety communications, field maintenance coordination, emissions reporting, project updates, executive briefings, and knowledge support across Microsoft 365 apps.

> **Important:** These exercises are designed for training and demonstration purposes. Replace all sample data, file names, assets, and business scenarios with approved company training content before delivering the labs in a production or customer environment.

## Participant Workbook Instructions

Use this workbook during the instructor-led session to follow each lab, capture your prompts, record Copilot outputs, reflect on results, and identify how the workflow could apply to your own company role or business process. The exercises use sample Enterprise-style scenarios and should be completed with approved training data only.

## How to Use This Workbook

- Read the scenario before starting each lab.
- Copy or adapt the suggested prompts into the relevant Microsoft 365 app.
- Record useful outputs, observations, and improvement ideas in the workbook sections.
- Use the reflection questions to evaluate whether Copilot output is accurate, complete, safe, and suitable for the intended audience.
- Do not enter confidential, restricted, personal, or safety-sensitive production data unless explicitly approved for the training environment.

## Capstone Activity

For the final activity, learners work in small groups to create an end-to-end operational readiness package. Each group uses Copilot Chat to brainstorm the briefing, Word to draft the report, Excel or Analyst agent to analyze sample data, PowerPoint to create the executive deck, Teams to prepare a meeting recap, Outlook to draft stakeholder communication, and Agent Builder or Copilot Studio to design a simple knowledge-support agent.

- One-page operational readiness briefing
- Excel insight summary or Analyst agent output
- Five-slide executive presentation
- Meeting follow-up message with decisions, risks, and actions
- Short agent concept with scope, knowledge sources, test prompts, and governance notes
