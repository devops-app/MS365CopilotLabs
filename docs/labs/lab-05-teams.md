# Lab 5 - Prepare and follow up on a BP operations meeting with Copilot in Teams

[← Previous: Lab 4](lab-04-powerpoint.md) | [Back to Home](../index.md) | [Next: Lab 6 →](lab-06-outlook.md)

## Instructor notes

This lab should highlight meeting preparation, in-meeting support, and post-meeting follow-up. Remind learners that Copilot summaries depend on available meeting data and should be checked before distributing actions or decisions.

## Scenario

Imagine you are joining a Teams meeting for a BP refinery turnaround readiness review. The meeting includes discussion about contractor readiness, safety permits, inventory, maintenance windows, and decision points for leadership escalation.

## Lab steps

1. Before the meeting, open the Teams meeting chat or meeting details.
2. Ask Copilot:

   > Help me prepare for this meeting. Summarize the agenda, expected decisions, unresolved questions, and documents I should review.

3. During or after the meeting, ask Copilot:

   > Summarize the key discussion points, decisions, risks, and action items from this meeting.

4. Ask Copilot:

   > Create a follow-up message for the turnaround readiness team with action owners, due dates, and escalation items.

5. Review the output and confirm all safety-sensitive or operationally critical details against the official meeting record.

## Debrief questions

- Did Copilot correctly separate decisions, risks, and actions?
- Which actions required confirmation?
- How can the follow-up message be made clearer for contractors, HSE teams, or leadership?
