# Lab 6 - Supercharge Company email workflows with Copilot in Outlook

[← Previous: Lab 5](lab-05-teams.md) | [Back to Home](../index.md) | [Next: Lab 7 →](lab-07-agents.md)

## Instructor notes

Use this lab to demonstrate inbox triage, thread summarization, drafting, tone adjustment, and stakeholder communication. Emphasize that learners must review recipients, attachments, commitments, and operational claims before sending any Copilot-generated message.

## Scenario

Imagine your inbox contains multiple email threads about a company terminal maintenance issue, including updates from operations, HSE, procurement, and contractor teams. You need to summarize the discussion, draft a professional response, and schedule a follow-up meeting if required.

## Lab steps

1. Open Outlook and select the relevant email thread.
2. Select Summarize with Copilot.
3. Review the summary and ask:

   > Identify the main issue, decisions made, unresolved questions, and action items from this thread.

4. Use Draft with Copilot and enter:

   > Draft a professional response to the terminal maintenance stakeholders. Confirm the current status, acknowledge safety and operational priorities, request missing information, and propose a 30-minute follow-up meeting.

5. Use coaching or rewrite options to make the message concise, collaborative, and suitable for senior stakeholders.
6. Review the draft carefully before sending.

## Debrief questions

- Was the drafted response appropriate for the audience?
- Did it overcommit on actions or timelines?
- What language should be adjusted for safety, compliance, or senior stakeholder communication?
