# Lab 7 - Use built-in Microsoft 365 Copilot agents for BP business insight

[← Previous: Lab 6](lab-06-outlook.md) | [Back to Home](../index.md) | [Next: Lab 8 →](lab-08-agent-builder.md)

## Instructor notes

Position the Researcher agent as a way to synthesize knowledge and the Analyst agent as a way to explore structured data. Clarify that learners should inspect sources, verify assumptions, and avoid using agents to make safety-critical decisions without review.

## Scenario

In this lab, you use the Researcher agent and Analyst agent to synthesize communications, documents, and data for a BP-style business topic. Use approved training content or your own permitted organizational data only.

## Lab steps

1. Open Microsoft 365 Copilot and select the Researcher agent.
2. Enter:

   > Help me gather and summarize recent discussions, documents, and emails related to BP refinery turnaround readiness from the past 90 days. Organize the output by key decisions, risks, stakeholders, and next steps.

3. Review the sources and refine the prompt:

   > Create a one-page executive briefing for a regional operations leader.

4. Open the Analyst agent.
5. Upload or reference the BP-style operational dataset.
6. Enter:

   > Analyze the relationship between maintenance hours, safety observations, throughput, and emissions estimate. Identify trends, anomalies, and recommended management actions.

7. Ask the Analyst agent to create suitable visuals or a stakeholder-ready summary.

## Debrief questions

- Which agent was more useful for the task?
- Did the Researcher agent cite or surface the right materials?
- Did the Analyst agent explain trends clearly enough for non-technical stakeholders?
